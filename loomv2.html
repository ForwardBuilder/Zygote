Welcome to The Epistemic Loom
1

What is the purpose of this simulation?

The Epistemic Loom is an interactive 3D mental sandbox and semantic thought-mapper. It visualizes abstract ideas, concepts, or philosophical questions as a self-organizing constellation of glowing crystalline nodes, helping you map out complex connections between thoughts.

2

How to interact?

Type any keyword or concept into the Concept Weaver sidebar to manifest a new 3D crystal node. Click and drag in space to orbit the camera, scroll to zoom, and click directly on any crystal node to inspect its resonance and entanglements.

3

Window Controls

You can minimize or maximize any panel using the top header bars for an unobstructed view of your 3D thought universe.
